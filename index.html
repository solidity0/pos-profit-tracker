    Business

    Sign in with this or your email. 3–30 letters, numbers, dots, dashes or underscores, not already used on Ledger. Leave it empty and save to remove it.

    Team

    Everyone who can sign in to this ledger. Names show next to each transaction. Removing someone blocks them straight away; their past entries keep their name. After removing someone you can delete them permanently, which takes them off this list for good.
    Add a salesperson
    They can sign in with the username or the email. Password reset links go to the email. Usernames are 3–30 letters, numbers, dots, dashes or underscores, and can't already be used on Ledger.
    They already have a login?
    Find their User UID in Firebase → Authentication → Users, and paste it here with their name above.

    Backup

    Downloads every transaction this business has ever logged — everyone's, including deleted ones (marked) — as a spreadsheet that opens in Excel or Google Sheets. Keep a copy somewhere safe, e.g. once a week.

    Old ledger (before businesses)

    Your ledger from before the switch to separate businesses is kept untouched as a backup. If a phone was still on the old version and logged something there, this copies across anything missing. Nothing gets duplicated or overwritten.

    Moniepoint rates

    Currently set to exact 0.5%. Use "override fee" on the entry form if a specific transaction doesn't match what Moniepoint actually shows. Table below updates live with whatever rates and auto-save amount you set above.

    OPay rates

    VAT on machine fee

    Confirmed as Transfer-only: your statement shows ₦1.50 VAT on every ₦20 transfer fee (exactly 7.5%), but no VAT line at all on Withdrawal/Purchase credits — their "Our fee" figure is the full deduction, VAT-free. Rate is set per-provider above — set a provider's VAT rate to 0 to turn it off.

    Stamp Duty (EMTL)

    Confirmed on your statement for Transfers ≥ ₦10,000 (₦50 flat). Whether withdrawals also attract it isn't confirmed yet — leave off unless you see it on a withdrawal line too. Set the flat charge to 0 to turn stamp duty off entirely.

    Bill Payments (Electricity, DSTV, GOTV, StarTimes)

    Your record book shows a dash under MC for Electricity/GOTV rows — no machine charge on bill payments — so this defaults to ₦0 and your full fee is profit. Edit if that ever changes.

    Betting

    Betting top-ups default to ₦0 machine fee, same as other bill payments. Edit if your terminal actually charges something for it.
