Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Ledger collects, why, and your rights over it. Ledger is provided by Triumph Enterprise ("we", "us"), the data controller for the account information described below. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). Contact us at victoryuweribe25@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: the business name, the owner's name and email address, salespeople's names and email addresses, and each login's ID.
  • Business records: the transactions you log (provider, type, amounts, fees and charges, profit, date and time, who logged them, and whether and when they were deleted), and your fee rates, tips and other settings.
  • Agreement records: when a business owner accepted these terms and which version.
  • On your device: the app keeps some things in your browser's storage (for example the calculator history, theme and text size, and an offline copy of your records so it works without internet). These stay on that device and are not used to track you.

We don't use advertising or analytics trackers, and we don't sell personal data.

2. Why we use it (and our legal basis)

  • To provide the Service: create and secure accounts, store and show your records to your team, and sync between devices. This is necessary for our contract with you.
  • To keep the Service safe: prevent abuse, investigate misuse, and suspend accounts that break the Terms of Use. This is our legitimate interest.
  • To meet legal obligations, where the law requires us to keep or share information.

3. Who can see your data

  • Your business: the owner sees all of the business's records. Salespeople see the business's transactions and reports, but not the owner-only settings.
  • Other businesses: never. Each business's data is kept separate, and our security rules block access across businesses.
  • Us: our admin tools show business names, owners' names and emails, and team lists, so we can manage accounts. As the operator we also have technical access to the database through Google's tools. We only look at transaction records when needed to run, secure or support the Service, or when required by law.
  • Usernames: so salespeople and business owners can sign in with a username, the email behind a username can be looked up by anyone who types that exact username. Usernames can't be listed or searched.
  • Our service provider: Google (Firebase) hosts the database and handles sign-in for us, acting on our instructions.

4. Where your data is stored

Records are stored by Google Cloud / Firebase in the United Kingdom (London, europe-west2). Sign-in data is processed on Google's global infrastructure. Where personal data leaves Nigeria, we rely on Google's contractual and security safeguards, as the NDPA allows.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your business account is active. If you ask us to delete your business's data, we'll delete it within 30 days, except anything we must keep by law. Removing a salesperson keeps their name on past entries, so the business's records stay complete.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules that limit each person to their own business and role. No system is perfectly secure, so use a strong password and keep it private.

7. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask to access your personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or receive a copy in a portable format. You can also withdraw consent where we rely on it. Salespeople can ask their business owner first; anyone can contact us at victoryuweribe25@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

8. Children

Ledger is for businesses and isn't meant for anyone under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We'll change the date above, and for important changes we may ask business owners to accept the new version.
